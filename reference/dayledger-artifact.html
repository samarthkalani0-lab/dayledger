Dayledger

Connect your calendar.

Dayledger turns the events in your own Google Calendar into today’s to-do list. Access is read-only, and everyone connects their own calendar, so nobody else can see yours.

  1. Sign in with the Google account (the email) your calendar uses. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and add Google Calendar. Google’s own sign-in screen opens there.
  2. Come back here and press Connect.
  3. We check today and tell you how many events we found.

You only see this page the first time you sign in. You can sync your calendar any time later with the Sync button.
